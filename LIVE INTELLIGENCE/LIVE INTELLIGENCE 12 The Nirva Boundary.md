\[
P_a \approx 1 - \exp\left( -\frac{\lambda \cdot D}{R_g + \varepsilon} \right)

- \(\lambda\) = threat density along the riverbank  
- \(D\) = distance through unsecured territory  
- \(R_g\) = Guard-to-Asset Ratio  
- \(\varepsilon\) = residual cover constant

### 3. The Simulation Perspective — Maung Nirva’s Protocol

In a virtual environment such as ARMA 3, hazard mapping carries zero real-world cost. A test convoy can be run through Sinkhan or Second Defile bottlenecks, every ambush point recorded, the mission restarted, and ground guard posts pre-positioned. When threat variables are static and visible upon replay, protection forces can be allocated with high precision and survival rates can approach 100% *inside the closed model*.

**Key simulation advantages:**
- Trial-and-respawn learning (failure is educational, not fatal)  
- Deterministic replay and controlled variables  
- Explicit hazard mapping of elevated ground and chokepoints  
- Modding extensibility (ACE3, TFAR/ACRE, custom terrains and scripts) turns the sandbox into a controlled laboratory

### 4. The Intelligence & Ground Reality Check (BEJ 74 / SSISM)
