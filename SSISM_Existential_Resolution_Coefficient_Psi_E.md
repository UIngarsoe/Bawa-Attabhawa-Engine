The ingested textual sources analyze four classic human responses to systemic anxiety and mortality:

Fearful Contemplation — Uncontrolled expansion of noise (S).
Stoic & Rational Acceptance — Direct engagement via awareness (C) and integrated purpose (K_int).

4. Operational Implications
Within the SSISM decision architecture, Ψ_E functions as a clarity governor.
When systemic noise rises, the exponential term in the denominator increases, reducing resolution unless courage and internal knowledge integration rise proportionally.
Avoidance (A) is treated as a suppressible term: the model seeks the limit as ( A \to 0 ), i.e., the state in which illusion and distraction no longer dominate the system.
This formalization allows the framework to treat stoic acceptance and purpose not as soft moral advice, but as measurable contributors to decision quality under uncertainty.
