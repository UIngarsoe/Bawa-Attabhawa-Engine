1. Executive Summary — Narrative Engineering vs. Observable Reality
This Civil Intelligence briefing examines the gap between institutional defensive claims and independently observable consequences, using the reported 10–11 September 2026 Tada-U (Mandalay) airbase incident as a teaching case.
The purpose is not operational instruction.
The purpose is epistemic discipline: how an analyst separates declared capability from demonstrated performance.
Core finding:
No single electronic countermeasure layer should be treated as a universal, deterministic guarantee against every threat class and operating condition. Claims of 100% interception must be tested against structural consequences (flight suspensions, asset relocation, visual telemetry) and not accepted solely on institutional confidence.
2. FPV Technology Classes — Conceptual Taxonomy
For civil-intelligence understanding (not operational guidance), contemporary first-person-view systems may be grouped into four conceptual classes:
Class
Description

Operator control via radio link and video stream on common bands

Analytical point: A defense optimized for one class does not automatically neutralize the others. Architectural diversity matters.
3. Mathematics of Signal Decay — Conceptual Baseline
Free-space path loss provides a useful conceptual baseline. Received power declines with the square of distance:
[ P_r \propto \frac{1}{d^2} ]
A simplified signal-to-noise comparison between controller and jammer may be written:
[ \text{SNR} \approx \frac{P_{\text{controller}} / d_{\text{controller}}^2}{P_{\text{jammer}} / d_{\text{jammer}}^2} ]
Important Academic Refinement
Inverse-square law is a propagation baseline, not a complete ECM model. Real performance also depends on:
antenna gain and orientation
terrain and structures
frequency and polarization
interference environment
system architecture
[ \boxed{\text{Inverse-Square Law} \neq \text{Complete ECM Model}} ]
Additional conceptual limits of single-layer soft-kill defenses include:
terminal kinetic momentum after link loss
frequency coverage gaps
time asymmetry between detection and physical impact once a vehicle is already in terminal dive
