2. Classroom 66 — Original Case Study
Context
A Burmese medical doctor operating under the Civil Disobedience Movement (CDM) transitioned to baking and selling traditional Burmese pastries to sustain livelihood while addressing widespread psychological exhaustion among the public.
Analytical Core
The practitioner acknowledges that while physical matter (sweetness / aroma) can temporarily induce localized positive sensation, complex cognitive states (depression / demoralization) cannot be cured solely by physical consumption. It requires an institutional fallback—a structured counseling engine.

Q2 — Systemic Safety Net
The doctor links pastry to tele-health counseling when morale does not rise.
